This Privacy Policy explains how Oops! Stickman handles information. The game is developed for offline play and does not require an account.

Information We Collect

Oops! Stickman stores gameplay progress locally on your device, such as unlocked levels, star ratings, and sound or music settings. This local data is used only to provide the game experience and is not sent to our server by the core gameplay.

Advertising And Tracking

The app may include advertising SDKs. Where required by iOS, the app may ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing tracking-related identifiers. If permission is denied, the app should continue to work without personalized tracking.

Third-party advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate region, app interaction data, or diagnostics for advertising delivery, fraud prevention, measurement, and compliance, depending on your consent and applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Oops! Stickman is a casual puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe personal information has been collected from a child, please contact us so we can review the request.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by third-party service providers used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, crash diagnostics, or platform compliance when those services are included in the app build.

Offline Gameplay

The main game experience is playable offline. Some advertising, measurement, or platform services may require network access when available, but they are not required to complete the core puzzle levels.

Data Retention

Local gameplay progress remains on your device until you delete the app or clear the app's data. Third-party service retention is governed by the policies of the relevant providers.

Your Choices

You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. You can also delete local game data by deleting the app from your device.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or when legal requirements change. The updated version will be identified by the date shown at the top of this page.
